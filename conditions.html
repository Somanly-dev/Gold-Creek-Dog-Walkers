Which Dogs We Can Walk

We want every walk to be safe and fun, for your dog and for us!

✅ Great fit

Small and medium-sized dogs.

👍 Usually fine

Big dogs who are calm and easy to handle on a leash.

⚠️ Not a good fit

We don't walk very strong dogs, like ones that pull hard on the leash. We want to keep everyone safe, so we only take dogs we can handle well.
